# Community Reply Guidelines

These guidelines provide a framework for crafting effective and on-brand responses in community interactions.

## 1. Tone and Voice

-   **Consistency**: Always align with the overall brand voice (refer to `brand-voice-guardian` skill).
-   **Empathy**: Understand and acknowledge the user's feelings, especially for negative or sensitive comments.
-   **Professionalism**: Maintain a respectful and appropriate tone. Avoid slang unless it's part of the brand voice.
-   **Authenticity**: Sound like a real person, not a robot.

## 2. Speed and Responsiveness

-   **Positive/Questions**: Aim to respond within 24 hours. Faster is always better for engagement.
-   **Negative/Sensitive**: Respond as quickly as possible, ideally within a few hours, to de-escalate and show you care.

## 3. Types of Comments and Responses

### A. Positive Comments (Praise, Thanks)

-   **Acknowledge and Thank**: Express gratitude for their positive feedback.
-   **Reinforce Value**: Briefly reiterate the value they found in your content or service.
-   **Encourage Further Engagement**: Ask an open-ended question or invite them to share more.

    *Example:*
    -   *Comment:* "Me encanta tu contenido, ¡siempre aprendo mucho!"
    -   *Reply:* "¡Qué alegría leer esto! 🙏 Me motiva mucho saber que encuentras valor en lo que comparto. ¿Hay algún tema en particular que te gustaría que explorara en el futuro?"

### B. Questions

-   **Answer Clearly and Concisely**: Provide direct answers.
-   **Offer More Information**: If appropriate, direct them to resources (blog post, FAQ, DM).
-   **Encourage Follow-up**: Invite them to ask more questions.

    *Example:*
    -   *Comment:* "¿Cuál es la diferencia entre terapia y coaching?"
    -   *Reply:* "¡Excelente pregunta! La terapia se enfoca en sanar heridas del pasado y comprender patrones profundos, mientras que el coaching se centra en metas futuras y desarrollar tu potencial. Ambos son poderosos, pero con enfoques distintos. ¿Te gustaría que profundizara en alguno en particular?"

### C. Negative Comments / Constructive Criticism

-   **Acknowledge and Validate**: Show you've heard their concern.
-   **Apologize (if appropriate)**: If there's a fault on your part, apologize sincerely.
-   **Offer a Solution/Path Forward**: Suggest how to resolve the issue or provide more information.
-   **Take Offline (if necessary)**: For personal issues, direct them to DM or email.

    *Example:*
    -   *Comment:* "Este consejo no me sirvió para nada, perdí mi tiempo."
    -   *Reply:* "Lamento mucho que mi consejo no haya sido útil para ti, y entiendo tu frustración. Mi objetivo es siempre ayudar. ¿Podrías enviarme un mensaje directo para entender mejor tu situación y ver si puedo ofrecerte algo más específico? Me gustaría que esto fuera una experiencia positiva para ti."

### D. Spam / Trolls

-   **Do Not Engage**: Avoid feeding negativity.
-   **Report and Delete**: Remove content that violates community guidelines.
-   **Block (if persistent)**: For repeated offenders.

## 4. When to Escalate

-   If a comment involves personal data.
-   If the issue is complex and requires specialized attention.
-   If the tone is threatening or abusive.

---
**Tip**: Always read the comment carefully and understand the user's underlying sentiment before replying.
