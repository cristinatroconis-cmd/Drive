# SEO Best Practices for Content

Search Engine Optimization (SEO) helps your content rank higher in search results, bringing more organic traffic.

## 1. Keyword Research & Intent

-   **Identify Target Keywords**: Use tools (or internal knowledge) to find terms your audience searches for. Focus on long-tail keywords (3+ words) as they often indicate higher intent.
-   **Understand Search Intent**: What is the user *really* looking for when they type that query? (Informational, Navigational, Transactional, Commercial Investigation). Your content should match this intent.
-   **Primary vs. Secondary Keywords**: Choose one primary keyword for the content and a few related secondary keywords.

## 2. On-Page SEO Elements

-   **Title Tag**:
    -   Include the primary keyword, ideally at the beginning.
    -   Keep it concise (around 50-60 characters) and compelling.
    -   Make it descriptive and unique.
-   **Meta Description**:
    -   Summarize the content and include the primary keyword.
    -   Write it to encourage clicks (around 150-160 characters). It doesn't directly impact ranking but influences CTR.
-   **Headings (H1, H2, H3...)**:
    -   Use a single H1 tag for the main title, including the primary keyword.
    -   Use H2s and H3s to break up content and include secondary keywords naturally.
    -   Maintain a logical hierarchy.
-   **URL Structure**:
    -   Keep URLs short, descriptive, and include the primary keyword.
    -   Use hyphens to separate words.
-   **Content Quality & Length**:
    -   Provide comprehensive, valuable, and unique content. Aim for depth over superficiality.
    -   Content length often correlates with higher rankings, but prioritize quality.
    -   Naturally integrate keywords throughout the content, avoiding "keyword stuffing."
-   **Images & Multimedia**:
    -   Use descriptive file names and `alt` text for images (include keywords where relevant).
    -   Compress images for faster loading.

## 3. Readability & User Experience (UX)

-   **Easy to Read**: Use short sentences and paragraphs.
-   **Break Up Text**: Utilize bullet points, numbered lists, and bold text.
-   **Internal Linking**: Link to other relevant content on your site. This helps users navigate and distributes "link equity."
-   **External Linking**: Link to high-authority external sources when appropriate.
-   **Mobile-Friendliness**: Ensure the content displays well on all devices.
-   **Page Speed**: Faster loading times improve user experience and SEO.

## 4. E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness)

-   **Demonstrate Expertise**: Show you know your topic well.
-   **Be Authoritative**: Back up claims with evidence.
-   **Build Trust**: Be transparent and accurate.
-   **Show Experience**: Share real-world examples or personal insights where appropriate.

---
**Tip**: Focus on providing value to the user first; search engines reward high-quality, user-focused content.
