# Hashtag and SEO Optimizer

This skill focuses on optimizing content visibility through platform-specific SEO practices, primarily targeting search-friendly captions and strategic categorization.

## Workflow

1.  **Analyze Content**: Receive the draft post copy.
2.  **Generate SEO Captions**: Use the strategies defined in `references/seo_caption_strategies.md` to structure captions with a strong Hook + Keyword principal in the first 1-2 lines, followed by semantically rich content.
3.  **Optimize Reach**: Generate 3-5 specific, high-intent hashtags if needed for thematic categorization.

## Bundled Resources

-   `references/seo_caption_strategies.md`: Source of Truth (SSoT) for SEO-optimized caption structures and keyword protocols.
-   `references/niche_keywords.md`: A library of target keywords mapped to the Existentialist approach.
