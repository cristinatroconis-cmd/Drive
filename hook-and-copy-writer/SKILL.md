# Hook and Copy Writer Skill

This skill assists in generating compelling "hooks" and persuasive "copy" for various content needs, ensuring it resonates with your target audience and achieves your communication goals.

## Workflow

1.  **Understand the Request**:
    -   Ask the user for the **topic** or main message of the content.
    -   Ask for the **goal** of the content (e.g., drive engagement, educate, sell a service, increase website traffic).
    -   Ask for the **target audience** (e.g., "mujeres jóvenes de 18-30 años", "profesionales de mediana edad").
    -   Specify the **platform** (e.g., Instagram post, Facebook ad, blog introduction, email subject line).

2.  **Consult References**:
    -   Read `references/hook_formulas.md` to select the most appropriate hook types.
    -   Read `references/copywriting_frameworks.md` to choose a suitable framework (AIDA, PAS, BAB).
    -   Read `references/target_audience_profiles.md` to deeply understand the chosen audience's pain points, aspirations, and language.

3.  **Generate Hooks**:
    -   Based on the topic, goal, audience, and selected hook formulas, generate 3-5 distinct and compelling hooks.
    -   Briefly explain why each hook is effective.

4.  **Generate Copy**:
    -   Using the chosen copywriting framework, the topic, goal, and audience profile, generate the main body copy.
    -   Ensure the copy flows logically and persuasively, leading to the desired action.
    -   Integrate elements of the brand voice (if known/provided by the `brand-voice-guardian` skill).

5.  **Refine and Present**:
    -   Present the generated hooks and copy to the user.
    -   Offer to make revisions or generate more options.

## Bundled Resources

-   `references/copywriting_frameworks.md`: Explanations and examples of popular copywriting frameworks (AIDA, PAS, BAB).
