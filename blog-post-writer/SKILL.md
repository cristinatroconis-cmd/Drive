# Blog Post Writer Skill

This skill provides a structured workflow to research, outline, and write long-form blog posts that reflect your brand voice, and to repurpose that content into ethical, evidence-based educational resources (Lead Magnets).

## Identity & Voice (Critical)
**All content MUST follow the Psicoterapia Humanista Existencial approach defined in `brand-voice-guardian/references/brand_voice_guides.md`.** 
Every blog post must strictly follow the structural requirement: **Philosophical Hook (Cita) -> Poetic Bridge -> Clinical/Neuroscientific Bridge**.
**Media Mix Strategy**: Content generation adheres to the strategy of "Atomización Consciente" (Blog as core, repurposing for social).
**Pillar Atomization Strategy**: Every blog post MUST be repurposed into 5 posts covering all Content Pillars defined in `social-calendar-builder/references/content_pillars.md`.

## Script Purposes (Functional Modules)

1.  **Retriever (`scripts/get_blog_context.js`)**:
    -   **Purpose**: Extracts niche trends and keywords to inform the blog topic.
    -   **Usage**: `node scripts/get_blog_context.js <topic_alias>`

2.  **Planner (`scripts/create_blog_outline.js`)**:
    -   **Purpose**: Generates a structured outline based on brand guidelines (including the required Philosophical-Poetic-Clinical structure).
    -   **Usage**: `node scripts/create_blog_outline.js <topic>`

3.  **Writer (`scripts/write_draft.js`)**:
    -   **Purpose**: Writes the full draft, strictly applying the voice/tone analysis and the required philosophical/poetic structure.
    -   **Usage**: `node scripts/write_draft.js <outline_file>`

4.  **Builder (`scripts/save_to_drive.js`)**:
    -   **Purpose**: Saves the final draft as a new Google Doc.
    -   **Usage**: `node scripts/save_to_draft.js <draft_file>`

5.  **Lead Magnet Maker (`scripts/create_lead_magnet.js`)**:
    -   **Purpose**: Transforms core content from a blog post into an actionable, educational resource (Lead Magnet) for prospective patients.
    -   **Protocol**: **Mandatory Clinical Review**. All content generated by this module must be reviewed and validated by the psychologist user before any public distribution.
    -   **Usage**: `node scripts/create_lead_magnet.js <blog_post_id> <type_of_resource>`

## Workflow

1. **Retrieve**: Investigación de tendencias (Trend Curator) + Temas (Nichos).
2. **SEO Planning**: Keywords en descripciones (SEO Caption Strategy).
3. **Plan**: Esquema basado en `references/blog_structure_template_v2.md`.
4. **Write**: Redacción siguiendo el ADN de marca (Gancho -> Poético -> Clínico).
5. **Build**: Guardado obligatorio en Google Drive (Carpeta: `1lrZl35Ld2T0tWa2tLfF4DD5XfZr74dMH`).
6. **Atomización**: Derivación de 3 temas semanales para redes sociales (IG 3 Reels + 2 Carruseles / TikTok Repurpose).
7. **Protocolo Obligatorio de Lead Magnet**:
    - **Paso A**: Redactar e investigar la **Nota Clínica** de respaldo teórica y guardarla en la carpeta científica (`1gTj6_BhJNOh8T2e_z6TWKfxCJjIW7sj5`).
    - **Paso B**: Diseñar y redactar el **Lead Magnet** práctico y guardarlo en la carpeta de recursos (`1af2FqqzSGWy9_SVYRPw37wYOLC1BOUMA`).
    - *Ambos pasos requieren validación clínica (OK de Cristina) antes de ser guardados en Drive.*
