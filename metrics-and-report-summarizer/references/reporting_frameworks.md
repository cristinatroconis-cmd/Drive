# Reporting Frameworks for Data Analysis

These frameworks provide structured approaches to analyze data and derive meaningful insights from metrics.

## 1. SWOT Analysis (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats)

**Purpose:** A strategic planning technique used to identify internal (Strengths and Weaknesses) and external (Opportunities and Threats) factors that are favorable and unfavorable to achieving an objective.

-   **Strengths**: Internal capabilities and resources that give you an advantage.
    -   *Metrics focus:* High performing campaigns, viral content, strong organic reach, high website conversion rates.
-   **Weaknesses**: Internal limitations that place you at a disadvantage.
    -   *Metrics focus:* Low engagement on certain platforms, high bounce rate on key pages, low ad CTR, declining follower count.
-   **Opportunities**: External factors that the organization could exploit to its advantage.
    -   *Metrics focus:* Emerging social media trends, untapped keyword niches, new platform features, competitor weaknesses.
-   **Threats**: External factors that could pose a risk to the organization.
    -   *Metrics focus:* Declining platform reach, increased ad costs, new competitor activity, negative sentiment trends.

## 2. SMART Goals Review (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound)

**Purpose:** Evaluate performance against predefined goals, ensuring they were well-defined and providing clear feedback for future goal setting.

-   **Specific**: Is the goal clearly defined? (e.g., "Increase Instagram engagement rate by 15%")
-   **Measurable**: Can progress be tracked with metrics? (e.g., engagement rate, follower growth).
-   **Achievable**: Was the goal realistic given resources and constraints? (e.g., compare against historical performance or industry benchmarks).
-   **Relevant**: Does the goal align with overall business objectives? (e.g., does increasing engagement support lead generation?).
-   **Time-bound**: Was there a deadline? (e.g., "by end of Q3").
    -   *Metrics focus:* Compare actual metrics against target metrics for the specified period.

## 3. Benchmark Comparison

**Purpose:** Assess performance by comparing your metrics against industry averages, competitors, or your own historical data.

-   **Historical Comparison**: Compare current performance (e.g., this month's website traffic) against previous periods (last month, last quarter, last year) to identify trends.
-   **Competitor Comparison**: Benchmark key metrics against direct or indirect competitors (if data is publicly available or estimated).
-   **Industry Benchmarks**: Compare against industry averages for engagement rates, CTRs, conversion rates, etc.

## 4. Funnel Analysis

**Purpose:** Track user journey through a series of steps (e.g., awareness -> consideration -> conversion) to identify drop-off points and optimization opportunities.

-   **Awareness**: Impressions, Reach, Website Visits.
-   **Consideration**: Clicks, Engagements, Time on Site, Pages Viewed.
-   **Conversion**: Form Submissions, Purchases, Sign-ups, Leads.
    -   *Metrics focus:* Conversion rates between each stage of the funnel.

---
<b>Tip:</b> Combine these frameworks to get a holistic view of your performance.
