# Metrics Templates

This file provides common metrics to look for in various types of reports, helping to standardize analysis and ensure key performance indicators (KPIs) are considered.

---

## 1. Social Media Engagement Metrics (e.g., Instagram, Facebook)

-   **Reach**: Number of unique users who saw your content.
-   **Impressions**: Total number of times your content was displayed (can be higher than reach as one person can see it multiple times).
-   **Engagement Rate**: (Likes + Comments + Shares + Saves) / Reach (or Followers). Indicates how interactive your content is.
-   **Follower Growth**: Net change in followers over a period.
-   **Click-Through Rate (CTR)**: Clicks on links / Impressions. Relevant for stories, bio links, or ads.
-   **Video Views**: Number of times a video was played (often defined by a minimum duration, e.g., 3 seconds).
-   **Audience Demographics**: Age, gender, location of your audience.

---

## 2. Website Performance Metrics (e.g., Google Analytics)

-   **Sessions**: Total number of visits to your site.
-   **Users**: Number of unique visitors.
-   **Page Views**: Total number of pages viewed.
-   **Bounce Rate**: Percentage of single-page sessions (visits where the person left your site from the entrance page without interacting with the page).
-   **Average Session Duration**: How long users spend on your site.
-   **Traffic Sources**: Where your visitors are coming from (Organic Search, Social, Direct, Referral).
-   **Top Pages**: Which pages are most popular.
-   **Conversions**: Completion of a desired goal (e.g., form submission, purchase, email signup).

---

## 3. Ad Campaign Metrics (e.g., Facebook Ads, Google Ads)

-   **Spend**: Total amount of money spent on the campaign.
-   **Impressions**: Total number of times your ad was shown.
-   **Reach**: Number of unique users who saw your ad.
-   **Clicks**: Total number of clicks on your ad.
-   **CTR (Click-Through Rate)**: Clicks / Impressions.
-   **CPM (Cost Per Mille/Thousand Impressions)**: Cost to show your ad 1,000 times.
-   **CPC (Cost Per Click)**: Total spend / Clicks.
-   **Conversions**: Number of desired actions completed.
-   **Conversion Rate**: Conversions / Clicks (or Impressions).
-   **CPA (Cost Per Acquisition/Action)**: Total spend / Conversions.
-   **ROAS (Return On Ad Spend)**: Revenue from ads / Ad spend.

---
**Tip**: Always consider the context and your specific goals when analyzing these metrics.
